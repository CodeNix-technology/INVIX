# Features

## Invoice Builder

- **Three-pane layout**: Form, live preview, customize.
- **Line items**: drag-to-reorder, HSN/SAC, quantity, rate, unit, discount, tax.
- **Totals**: subtotal, tax breakdown, extra charges, round-off, grand total.
- **Payments**: partial payments, paid/balance tracking, payment terms, due date.
- **Business details**: multi-business profiles, logo, GSTIN, address, contact info.
- **Client details**: saved clients, billing details, GSTIN, contact info.
- **Notes & terms**: custom notes and terms/conditions.
- **Bank & UPI**: bank details and auto-generated UPI QR.
- **Signature/stamp**: draw/upload/type signature, company stamp.
- **Productivity**: autosave, undo/redo, duplicate invoice, quotation → invoice conversion.

## Customization Studio

- Colors and preset palettes
- Google fonts (heading/body)
- Logo size and position
- Section visibility and ordering
- Column manager (toggle/rename/reorder)
- Paper size (A4/A5/Letter), orientation, margins
- Watermark, labels, table style, borders/radius
- Save designs as **My Templates**
- Duplicate/rename/delete/export/import template designs (JSON)

## Export & Sharing

- **PDF export** (paginated)
- **PNG export**
- **Print**
- **Public share links**
- **WhatsApp/email handoff**
- **CSV/Excel/JSON** data exports

## Dashboard & Analytics

- KPI cards: invoiced, collected, pending, overdue, count, avg invoice value, collection rate
- Monthly revenue trends and comparisons
- Sales by client and product/service performance
- GST summary (CGST/SGST/IGST)
- Receivables aging and cashflow forecast
- Expense tracking and goals
- Notification center and overdue reminders

## Local-First & Cloud

- **Offline/guest mode**: IndexedDB local workspace, no sign-in required.
- **Optional Supabase**: auth, Postgres sync, storage, RLS policies.
- **Offline queue/auto sync**: handles sync gracefully.
- **Storage abstraction**: pluggable providers (Supabase/local/custom).

## UX & Platform

- Dark/light/system theme
- Responsive design (desktop/mobile)
- PWA installable with offline support
- Command palette and keyboard shortcuts
- i18n: English, Tamil, Hindi
- Accessible, consistent UI styling
